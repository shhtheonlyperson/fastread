# JustRead - Terms of Use

_Last updated: 2026-05-17_

These Terms of Use apply to the JustRead Chrome extension.

## What JustRead is

JustRead is a local reader-mode and speed-reading tool. It extracts the
article from the current browser tab, displays it in a cleaner reader view,
and can play it back one word or chunk at a time.

## Your content

You keep all rights to the pages, articles, notes, and other content you
open with JustRead. JustRead does not claim ownership of that content and
does not upload it to a JustRead server.

You are responsible for using JustRead only with content you are allowed to
access and read.

## Acceptable use

Do not use JustRead to break laws, bypass access controls, violate website
terms, or interfere with websites, browsers, devices, or other users.
