- **Learnings for future iterations:**
  - The Xcode project is regenerated from `project.yml` via xcodegen — do NOT hand-edit `FastRead.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj`. Add SPM deps in `project.yml`'s top-level `packages:` and reference them with `dependencies: - package: Name` per target. `sources: - path: Sources/FastReadCore` already auto-discovers all `.swift` files in the directory, so new core files no longer need pbxproj edits — they're picked up automatically on `xcodegen generate`.
  - `org.idpf.epub-container` is the canonical exported UTI for EPUB. `UTType.epub` is also available iOS 14+; passing both to `.fileImporter(allowedContentTypes:)` is harmless and gives broader file-source coverage.
  - When `.fileImporter` returns a URL from outside the app sandbox (Files app, share sheet), call `startAccessingSecurityScopedResource()` before reading and `stopAccessingSecurityScopedResource()` in `defer`. Without this, `Data(contentsOf:)` quietly fails on iOS for documents picked from iCloud Drive or other providers.
  - `ReadingStore.addEpubArticle(data:filename:)` reuses `addArticle(document:source:sourceURL:)` so chapter sections survive into storage via the existing custom `Codable` on `ReadingArticle` (which encodes both `document` and flattened `text`). Future stories that need chapter-aware playback can read `currentArticle.document.sections` directly.
  - Info.plist `CFBundleDocumentTypes` + `LSSupportsOpeningDocumentsInPlace=true` is the minimum for "Open in JustRead" from the share sheet. `UISupportsDocumentBrowser` is NOT needed (and would force a different app architecture).
