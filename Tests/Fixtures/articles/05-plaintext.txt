A short note on attention.

Attention is a finite resource that must be replenished, much like physical strength or working memory capacity. When we treat it as unlimited we end every day depleted, and the work that depended on careful thought becomes the work that suffered the most.

The honest move is to plan attention the way an athlete plans recovery. Decide ahead of time which problems deserve your sharp hours, and which can survive on your tired ones, and then protect that decision through the small interruptions that try to undo it.

Reading slowly is one way to practice this discipline. It forces you to notice when your mind has slipped away from the page, and it gives you a chance to bring it back before the meaning of the paragraph dissolves.
